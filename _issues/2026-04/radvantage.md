This is the hardest achievement in Hungry Giraffe. It is the one achievement that will break or make a mastery of this game. Since Supersize Me (10) exists you will likely get it while you grind for that achievement. A lot of the game is about memorisation and understanding its mechanics. Since the game lets you start between Stage 1 and Stage 10 (provided you have been there), it gives you a relative easy way to practise Stages you have problems with.

One thing to note is that you can start from say Stage 5 and earn the achievement when you reach Stage 15. Since Stage 11 basically is Stage 1, Stage 12 is Stage 2 etc it allows you to start with the hardest stages first. However, once you reach Stage 11, you can only hold up to 2 hard hats as opposed to the usual 3! This means you have less safety measures for those stages than if you start at Stage 1.

The achievement's main difficulty is how do you recover when you start to descend. If you're lucky there's a nearby chili pepper you can eat. Using a hard hat also works. But what can you do if there is no chili pepper in reach and you don't have any hard hats?

For some reason, this game has physic-based(!) eating mechanics. The more calories a food item has, the more does the giraffe's neck grow and ascend. If you eat more food in quick succesion, the giraffe ascends more than if the eaten food items were placed more sparse. If he is descending, the giraffe is rising less from eating a food item than if the giraffe is eating it while he ascends.

This means, if you are descending, it's better to eat from the bottom of a vertical line/cluster of food items and hope the momentum lets the giraffe recover. The game still relies a lot on memorisation. So practise makes perfect!
