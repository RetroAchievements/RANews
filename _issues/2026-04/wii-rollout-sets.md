            <td colspan=2>
Cave Story is a platforming, shooter-like game with some Metroidvania features, about a robot named Quote who wakes up alone in a Cave. As he explores the world around him, he learns of a race of rabbit-like creatures named Mimiga's who are being terrorized by an evil doctor and his two minions, Balrog and Misery. As you journey with Quote, you will discover a variety of different weapons that level up as experience chips are collected. Each weapon's level behaves slightly different than the last, giving the player such a unique experience to discover; however, watch out! As you take damage, so does your active weapon, causing it to level down and requiring you to level it back up again! As you travel through the various environments you will meet a broad cast of colorful characters, but good and bad, and a handful of quests. Be careful, you will also be faced against several large bosses that will challenge you Castlevania style, each with a unique method of attacks. Can you help Quote get his memories back again, learn the secrets of the floating island, and save the Mimiga's before they are completely taken over by the vile Doctor?<br>
<br>
Cave Story was one of the earliest most influential indie games during its time. This entire game was created by a single person, graphics, coding and music in all. It is packed with fun and memorial gimmicks, and unforgettable bosses. Cave Story hosts an experience you will not forget, and is still cherished by man to this day! It is argued that this is one of the most important early indie games that has since inspired a plethora of indie titles that came to follow, which were created by either a single person or small group, including myself. What makes it stand out is the unique weapons you can collect to test against enemies and bosses, and the combinations you can test around with, creating a decent amount of replayability. This game is certain to leave an impression on you.<br>
<br>
My goal was to create something that the other Cave Story sets were not already doing. I wanted to push the achievement features to their potential but using the different flags that are available to the developers, such as pushing L + R at the same time to view your current game percentage. There is a wide variety of challenges, none too hard, that will hopefully enhance the player experience. There are a good amount of puzzles that will get players trying to think their way to the solution. Like all my sets, this set include quite a few suggestions by the Cave Story community that I think they will be pleased to find. The set also hosts a color set of achievement badges, each were carefully created in order to provide a beautiful experience as the player is playing through the game and its various modes.<br>

So if the game is easy, how can we make it harder? Why, attempting to get high streaks in every level, of course!<br>
Every time you collect a bead, your streak counter goes up by 1. Every 50 on your streak gives you extra beads. Sounds simple, right? The catch, is that if you take damage, your streak goes back down to 0.<br>
<br>
But that's not all, as this game has a few tricks to get your streak even higher! If you defeat more than one enemy or weapons with a single projectile, they'll drop extra beads if it was their first death! You'll need to strategize how to deal with the enemies in the level to get the best combos, sometimes using an enemy more than once to get more beads from different enemies. In addition, you will need to find the best spot to collect a higher value bead so you can get hurt to turn that one big bead into multiple smaller beads for an even higher streak!<br>
<br>
To give you some guidance, I've included notes on every achievement from when I went through the levels of what combos I did, the number of beads I got before the damage trick and how high it became afterwards, along with other milestones throughout the levels. I wish anyone that dares to attempt this the best of luck, you WILL need it.
