## About

![Box art for the United States version of Dragon Quest 8](https://media.retroachievements.org/Images/062378.png)

**[Disclaimer]**

** ***This guide is written on the intention that you have already played the main set to its entirety and know of all possible spoilers.*** **

While most of the main storyline achievements are in order as [TheMysticalOne](https://retroachievements.org/User/TheMysticalOne) has them listed on RetroAchievements, some may be gotten at different times depending on your style of playing. This guide is how I personally played through the entirety of the game and is not intended to be a direct way you should play, only a suggested way in getting the achievements the fastest way I could find for 100% completion. If you are a casual player and do not care about mastering sets, I would suggest experiencing this subset without the use of my guide. If you are wanting to master the set then continue on. I will provide many notes and important things necessary to unlock each achievement. **Be sure to collect all items and treasures you come across, including the mini medals.** These are crucial to unlocking all achievements!

Please be aware that because all of these are one-time only chats, it should go without saying the entirety of the achievements on this set are **missable.** I would highly recommend making plenty of back-up saves during your adventure. While RetroAchievements has recently introduced the multi-set functionality, I would also advise that you play this particular subset on a new standalone file as some instances could interfere or lock you out of getting certain achievements on the main set. From my experience, it is far too difficult to keep up and manage all of the achievements to be playing this subset in conjunction with the main.

**One last thing – It should be obvious but you will need each character alive for their specific party chats. How are they supposed to chat if they’re dead? xD Anyhoo!**

## II. Main Storyline Achievements

As previously stated, these achievements are listed in order the fastest way I could get them and may not reflect the order listed on the subset achievement page itself. **I cannot stress this enough, but be sure to make plenty of additional save files** just in case you need to backtrack and get something you might have accidentally overlooked or skipped even with the use of my guide. This is a very lengthy game and missing just one achievement could cost you hours of time to go back and get. I will provide additional notes underneath specific achievements where creating a back-up save file is absolutely necessary. In comparison to the Battle Menu achievements, these will all be obtained by pressing the Start Button to open the party chat with who is currently in your party. **Again - make sure each member is alive and present** so you will earn the achievement for their specific chat dialogues. 

## <u>[Chapter I] Let's Start Chatting</u>




Our first chapter of the lengthy chat adventure begins at Farebury and will eventually end after the events at Maella Abbey. There will be a couple of achievements which are mandatory for skipping ahead without Angelo present in the party, but I will let you know when that occurs. For now, collect all items and battle every enemy you encounter to get a jump on gaining experience. Collect everything and win all of your fights! **There are a total of 9 Achievements in this first Chapter.**

## III. The Dragon Drubber Achievement

**The holy grail achievement of the Party Chat Subset.** With it being worth 50pts and only being gotten at the very end of the game, I figured to make this lone achievement its own section. If you are struggling, or have any difficulties, I would advise going back and farming more Metal King Slimes to further advance your party's overall stats. Make sure you have the best available equipment on hand - minus weapons.. cough. That will be explained momentarily..

You will know you are good to go when the Lord of the Dragovians gives a unique dialogue asking if you are certain you wish to battle him with no weapons equipped prior to the fight. I would store all weapons in your bag prior to the fight just in case. You are free to use healing items, healing spells, and stat buffs – nothing else. Using attacking abilities like Dragon Soul will cancel this out and you will not get the achievement. Psyching up and Timbrel of Tension is still allowed (and definitely recommended!)
