Extremely cool, but tough achievement where you need to recreate game that was featured in the OVA special the game was based on. This also serves as the final challenge to show off the techniques you've learned in the rest of the set.

<p align="center">
  <img width="720" src="https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/4602dacfaa9e.png"/>
</p>

Above is the scoreboard of the game you need to recreate. Select the east blue and strawhat pirate teams as the two teams in battle mode (the free play mode). Make sure you toggle the middle option so the strawhats are "second bat" (後攻) and are playing on the default field.

You need to give up runs to the other team in the following innings:

- 2 runs in the first inning
- 1 run in the fifth inning

Give these runs up as soon as possible by throwing the default pitches down the middle and letting the ball go for a homerun or have it roll into the outfield and making sure none of your outfielders pick up the ball before the hitter scores the run by going around all the bases.

Once you get those mandatory runs out of the way, don't throw any pitches to the middle of the strikezone. The opponent will hit auto homeruns at will if you play with fire like that and you'll need to restart the attempt. Instead, have the batter chase pitches on the outside and inside for easy Ks and pop flys.

When trying to score the 4 runs you need in the bottom of the 9th, PLAY SMALL BALL by placing bunts towards the first base line using the B button. The opponent pitcher's AI is dumb and won't throw fast enough to make an out 90% of the time. Either keep playing small ball to drive in runs or attempt to get a normal hit (no holding down L or R while hitting) to have the ball roll to the outfield while you push for runners to score with the L button.

Follow these tips and you should be able to pull off the comeback and get the achievement
