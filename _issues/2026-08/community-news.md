## New Patreon Tier

RetroAchievements has always been a non-profit and will always be. RA's backers have asked for another Patreon tier to open, and we want to provide something in return to make it worthwhile. Since the servers have been paid for and we haven't had concerns, there wasn't a need to investigate this, until lately. Prices for hosting have increased significantly due to surging global hardware costs, RAM supply shortages and rising energy expenses. Our hosting provider has mercifully grandfathered our server costs, however if overnight they were to charge us their going-rate, we wouldn't break even.

Rather than adding adverts, lowering the server capabilities, etc we don't want any of that. Instead we have decided to start looking at leaning on Patreon to see if we can invest in providing more content. To start this off, we are looking to add a new $2 Patreon Tier to allow you to be a "Super Supporter" https://www.patreon.com/retroachievements this will allow you to have a special HOLOGRAPHIC supporter badge, thanks to the incredible skills of {% rauser wes %} who has worked hard to support this and {% rauser SporyTike %} who has updated existing tiers on the site where possible.

## Softcore Renaming

"Softcore Mode" has been renamed to "Casual Mode". "Hardcore" is already a well-known term in gaming vocabulary even outside RA, featured prominently in games like Diablo. However, we've gotten in hot water with integration devs who are afraid to put the word "Softcore" in their apps (due to app store review), as this term is most commonly associated with adult content.

There are no plans to change Hardcore Mode naming.

# Set Design Team
The Set Design Team is looking for both players and developers with a solid grasp on set design that want to help make the site more enjoyable experience for our ever-growing community.

The core responsibilities of a Set Design member are as follows:
- Create revision plans for outdated, older sets to bring them up to a modern standard.
- Actively participate in team discussions, revision discussions, and general feedback threads.
- Assist developers who are seeking feedback for a set they are actively developing or plan to.
- Continue to provide feedback on subset requests and help determine which are appropriate.

The following restrictions apply:
- You must be in the top 1,000 Hardcore leaderboard, a current non-junior developer, or are a retired non-junior developer.
- You must be active on Discord and willing to work with Google Sheets, or at least until we have an on-site alternative.

In order to apply for the team, please fill out the following form: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfEdcL7HYIsCosdlgvspHlRqx3Jn9ztlc0nf7tJ3my0oC-p4Q/viewform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfEdcL7HYIsCosdlgvspHlRqx3Jn9ztlc0nf7tJ3my0oC-p4Q/viewform)

## Site Updates

### RAWeb 2026.08.26
**New Features**
- If you have a game with 0 achievements in your Want to Play list, when it gets a set, you'll now get an email notification.
- Active event achievements now get a marker on game pages. You can also turn on markers for evergreen achievements via a new setting.
- By popular request, sorting achievements on game pages no longer auto-groups your unlocked achievements first.

**Highlights for Developers**
- Untracked users without a team / developer role can no longer open tickets.
- Tickets opened against unsupported emulators (ie: AetherSX2) are now automatically quarantined.
- On achievement pages, "Logic updated" in changelogs now deeplinks to the Logic page in the management app.
- Game pages will no longer pop a quick completion warning for sole author revision claims.
- Logic authorship is now correctly preserved, regardless of who actually promotes an achievement.
- We are working on a number of updates to the ticket system that will be arriving soon.
