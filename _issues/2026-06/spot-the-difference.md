Summer, sun and samba music! In Sunny Town everyone celebrates and dances on the streets while listening to the funky rhythm of Amigo's maracas. Every day Amigo starts a new samba party in the morning and shakes his hands until the next day arrives. To ensure the people stay entertained he switches between a variety of different songs as well as playing them slightly differently every time. Due to him being busy most time of the day, he needs to plan the next party during the festivities of the day before. Can you help Amigo to keep track of the 10 things that changed this time so he can start planning for tomorrow?
