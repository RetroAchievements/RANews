# 2) Introduction

Harvest Moon was released on Super Nintendo in 1996 by Pack-In-Video and published overseas by Natsume and was a very unique title for the system. Rather than focus on action or platforming like its peers, you played the role of a farmer living in a quiet town in the mountains. It pioneered the farming genre and introduced us to the coziness of taking care of crops and animals while managing your social skills, befriending villagers and possibly starting a family with one of them. The series would later find success with subsequent entries and is still ongoing to this day, also being the inspiration for the mega hit Stardew Valley.

But, like most pioneers, the gameplay was refined with sequels and, despite this game being incredibly fun, it has some outdated gameplay mechanics and rough edges that you'll have to bear in mind while going for the completion. There is no rucksack in this game so every crop and item has to be carried by hand to either your shipping box, your horse or your intended gift recipient. Anything added to your shipping bin after 5 PM will be forever lost and there's only 4 crops in the entire game, making two of the seasons very slow paced outside of animal care.

Unfortunately, one limitation also applies to the scoring system which is counter intuitive to playing this game naturally. While most farm games expect you to ship as many crops as possible and befriend all of their virtual denizens, due to a programming glitch you will lose points if you raise some of these values above a certain limit. In order to get the maximum ranch master score, you will have to play within these limits and focus on getting each bachelorette and crop shipped to a maximum of 511.

To get every achievement in the game, we will have to obtain every ending, marry every possible candidate and obtain a maximum rancher score of either 994 or 999, depending on which style of gameplay you wish to go for. For this guide, we will be going for the maximum score of 999 which will also help give some leeway if you're off by a couple points but keep in mind any score between 994 and 999 will unlock the Master Rancher achievement. To ensure we don't have to replay large chunks of the game, we will be making backups of the save files we create while playing this game. This will ensure everything goes smoothly for master rancher but also facilitate remarrying each of the available bachelorettes and getting each of their unique endings.

Ultimately, we will be playing through this game roughly 2 and handful times and this guide will give you a walkthrough for each playthrough. The first time will be playing through the game until Year 3 and getting the maximum ranch master score along with a bunch of miscellaneous achievements along the way. We will reload our appropriate backup save file in order to propose to the other four bachelorettes and get each of their exclusive endings as well as an exclusive bachelor ending that requires a certain affection from the bachelorettes. Then, we will reload our earliest backup in order to focus on getting 800+ happiness and get the Happy Bachelor ending, which will take a few seasons. Our final playthrough will be easy as we will sleep until we obtain the Complete Failure ending.

There are some great resources for this game available on the internet. Harvest Moon Paradise features a ton of useful information regarding liked gifts and values, event information and tool upgrades. You can also check out the amazing FAQ written by The_Admiral. However, most of the information will be contained in this walkthrough but they make great additional resources to keep open, especially when you're ready to get the bachelorettes to to the specific 511 value.

## Breaking Down Ranch Master

Before we get into the game, we should break down exactly what is expected from getting Ranch Master score as that will be our primary focus during the first playthrough. There are two possible maximum scores: 994 and 999, both of which will get you the Ranch Master achievement despite the description showing only 994. Any value between these two will also unlock the achievement so if you go for 999 and your cow affection isn't maxed, you can still unlock the achievement with any score above 994. Which maximum score you go for depends on how you want to play this game. If you are going for 999, this is considered a maximum score run, which is the focus of this walkthrough. If you go for a score of 994, it's considered a maximum relationship run or a 100% run, in which all girls are at 999 affection. Unfortunately, the way this game calculates points means you can only get maximum score in blocks of 512 so you'll be just shy of the max score of 1009-1023, which is not possible due to the game's hard limit of 999 and you'll lose exactly one point for each bachelorette, hence the score of 994. You can choose to go for the maximum relationship run if you like but it will be slightly more work to get the bachelorettes to maximum, it will require more daily maintenance with your future wife and you will lose the 5 point wiggle room for cow affection if you're only interested in the Ranch Master achievement so bear that in mind. Below, you'll find a chart breaking down each part of maximum ranch master score.

The big thing you'll have to focus on while going for master rancher is the bachelorettes and crops as they are the only scores which are bugged to have a maximum of 511. While the bachelorettes can be lowered by giving them weeds or poison mushrooms, crops must be within that 496-511 range due to how the game calculates it. Unfortunately, going over 511 will cause the game to reset your ranch master points for that particular crop back to 0 and will ruin your ranch master run. While playing this set, you MUST keep an eye on these numbers either through rich presence, recommended as it's live updating, or the ending code to ensure you don't go over 511 crops or affection points aside from the bachelorette you intend to marry. The rest of the numbers are safe to surpass and max out and we will be doing so during this walkthrough.

While on your farm, you can check your rich progress either on the site or using RASNES and enabling rich presence live monitor. If you are checking via your profile page on the site, make sure you pause the emulator for a few minutes and refresh the site to ensure you're seeing the most accurate numbers. The rich presence will appear under your main profile picture in the section entitled "Most Recently Played". If you are at the farm, it will say "Jack is at the farm" and you will see the following emoji:

🥬 0 🥔 0 🌽 0 🍅 0

These represent Turnips, Potatoes, Corn and Tomatoes in that order.

Similarly, Bachelorettes' affection can be checked by heading in town. The rich presence will update with "Jack is in town" with the following emoji:

⛪ 0 🧰 0 🌻 0 🐄 0 🌳 0

These represent Maria, Ann, Nina, Ellen and Eve in that order.

Happiness will be covered in a separate section.

For animals, cows are your main priority. They will need to be fed every single day or you will take a hit to both your happiness stat and your cow affection. If you forget to feed them, they will take a -15 hit to their affection rate which will take 4 days of brushing and talking to them to recover. If you continue to forget, they will get sick which will further decrease your happiness and if they die, you'll take a whopping -100 points to your happiness stat. Interestingly enough, the game only cares that you feed the cows. For chickens, once you have 12, you can safely ignore them for the rest of the game if you so choose. They will neither get sick nor will they drop your happiness stat if they are not fed, they will simply stop laying eggs for three days until they are fed again.

Development rate is how much of your field has grass, crops or fences on it. In order to max out that stat, you will have to cover your entire field with either of these three. It's highly recommended to use grass to increase your development rate but please be aware that there is no way to remove grass once planted so if you still require crops for master rancher, there is no way to plant them if you've planted grass there already outside of a hurricane or the transition from winter into spring which destroys random tiles. Crops can increase your score but no longer count once harvested or wilted after fall and fences have no positive purpose so they should be discarded immediately.

The rest of the conditions will be self explanatory and covered in the guide.

## Bachelorettes

Within this game, there are five bachelorettes you can court and marry. For a perfect ranch master score, we will have to get all 5 to affection scores between 496-511 as shown in the ending and on rich presence. You will have to marry one of them and within a certain time frame in order to have both children. While you can theoretically wait until the end of Year 1, I recommend getting married by the end of summer, as you will have two anniversaries and be right on time to have both children in Year 2. Anniversaries allow you to get +30 happiness for EACH gift you give your wife that day which will hugely increase your happiness stat so having two will guarantee you can max it out if you're still not at maximum happiness by the end of Year 2. One thing to also keep in mind is that unlike other Harvest Moon games, villagers do not have affection scores, only bachelorettes so you shouldn't gift them anything outside of scripted events.

Like in most Harvest Moon games, you increase your relationships by talking with people and giving them gifts. While you can still do that in this game, it's much easier to take advantage of an exploit in the game's programming to quickly raise each bachelorette's affection. Every girl in the game will ask you various questions at specific times which change their affection score for the positive. Depending on the choice they give you, you will usually get either 8 points of affection or 2 depending on your answer though some vary. After this, they will temporarily stop asking you that particular question and change their dialogue. However, you can run into the nearest building and back out, talk to the bachelorette again until they repeat the question again and continue choosing the +8 dialogue option, allowing you to get the bonus over and over. Utilizing this strategy, you can get 80 affection points after just 10 times of doing this and you can do this enough times to get the girls in max range within a single day. Often times, you can max out multiple girls in a single day. Bear in mind that they cycle between asking the question and saying dialogue, such as Ellen comparing raising animals to being a mother, so if they're not asking the question that gives you affection points, keep talking to them until they do. If you want to supplement the question method to gain additional points, you can also take advantage of the fact that this game allows you to give the bachelorettes multiple gifts per day. Keep in mind that there is no relationship decay in this game so if you get a girl to 511 affection, so long as you don't interact with that person anymore, it will remain at that score forever. Also bear in mind while playing that the flower festival and harvest festival will increase the affection of the bachelorettes when you talk to them and dancing with them will give you a flat 30 points with that girl. For that reason, it's recommended that you only dance with the woman you intend to marry. Also, in Years 2 and 3, the only festivals you're required to go to are the egg festival, for the remaining prize, star night festival and new years so feel free to skip the rest of the festivals to protect your affection scores.

The affection score for gifts also change depending on when and where you give them to the bachelorettes and if you're married to them. For example, if you give Ann a mushroom, it will boost her relationship by 6 points if she's inside the tool shop. However, if she's at church or outside the tool shop, it will only boost it by 5 points. If you give it to her after you get married, she will not like it and you'll get 0 points.

If you go over the 511 affection point threshold, you can give weeds and poison mushrooms to the bachelorettes to correct this. Weeds will subtract 2 points each time, poison mushrooms will subtract 8, though for Nina it will only subtract 4 and will get you points if you're married to her so bear that in mind.

## Marriage and Kids

Once you get 255 affection or above with a bachelorette and you complete the first house upgrade, the peddler will start selling the blue feather for 1000g on Sundays. You can use this item to propose to the bachelorette of your choice as long as their affection is above that threshold. However, for some odd reason, the women will not accept unless it's a specific day of the week and it's sunny outside and will tell you as much when you attempt to propose. For every girl except Nina, this must be on a Saturday. For Nina, it needs to be on Sunday. Once she accepts, you will immediately get married and the day will end so be sure to take care of the animals and the crops prior to proposing. Write down the date of your marriage as you can use your anniversary to boost your happiness later!

Once you're married, each woman will change their schedule and what gifts they like. In the mornings and evenings, they'll hang around inside your house.  However, during the day, they'll go different places on the farm depending on who you married. For example, Ann will go in the tool shed to work on her inventions while Ellen will stand near the horse stable. They will head back in the house around 3 PM and fall asleep at 6 PM.

You will also gain and lose affection points depending on when you go to bed after you're married. If you go to bed when your wife is in the house, you'll gain +2 to your affection. This does not apply to Eve who will get -2 affection if you go to bed with her so keep that in mind if you want to marry her as you'll spend more time giving her gifts to keep her affection high. If you go to bed alone in the middle of the day while your wife is out of the house, you will get no points. If you instead wait till after 6 PM to go to bed, you will get -2 affection each time you enter the house, not when you go to bed. This is very useful for correcting your wife's affection points late in the game but is something to bear in mind as you'll have to give her gifts to correct it if you're attempting to have your first and second child.

Your wife can leave you if you're not careful and let her relationship score drop below 200 points which shouldn't be an issue if you're following this guide. This will give you -50 points to your happiness. When they move out, look for them in their original room. For example, check the back of the bar for Eve or the mayor's house for Maria. Talk to them and they will forgive you and return to the farm, netting you a happiness boost of 100 points.

Once you get the second house upgrade, have been married for at least 20 days and have 450 points of affection or higher, your wife will get pregnant and inform you if you talk to her. You do not have to talk to her for the pregnancy to count so if you do not talk to her every morning, the due date will likely be off from your calculations. From there, it will take 60 days for the child to be born. You'll know it's time because the priest will be blocking the doorway when you wake up and he'll mention you're going to be a father soon. Speak with your wife and she will offer to let you go work. If you say no, you will gain 50 affection points but you will not be able to feed your animals or take care of any crops. If you say yes, there will be no penalty so be sure to do so to take care of your chores unless you really need the affection boost. When you return, your child will be born and you'll have a chance to name them. The game keeps track of this birthday like your anniversary but the happiness boost is much smaller.

Your child will grow twice: once after 30 days in which your baby will start crawling and again after another 30 days in which they'll start walking and wearing a cow outfit. After 31 days have passed since the first birth and you have at least 650 affection points with your wife, she will get pregnant again. This will be your final child so after this announcement, you can safely lower your wife's affection back into the 496-511 range. You will need both children for a perfect ranch master score and to get both achievements. Keep in mind that you will get the ranch master points for the second child as well as the relevant ending as soon as your wife is pregnant with them.

Anniversaries are the best way to max out your score while married. By the time your first anniversary rolls around, you will likely have 12 chickens. Give all of your eggs to your wife and you will get +360 happiness in a single day! If you are not married, visit the mountain in spring and winter every day it is not raining, snowing or a festival and look for the animals. The squirrel is near the entrance to the mountain behind the log near the carpenter's house. The fox spawns near the hot spring. If they do not show up, you can run to either the cave, mountain top or crossroads and try again to get it to spawn until it becomes night time and no longer spawns. You can see it once per day.

You can also breed cows for 70 points per cow but this is only recommended for the Happy Bachelor ending. If you do this, be sure to purchase the medicine and give it to your cow when you give it the miracle potion. This will make the pregnancy go from 21 days to just 2 days. If you breed the entire barn, you can get a whopping 770 happiness points, provided you can afford the 55,000g to purchase all the potions and medicine.

## Endings

This game has a total of 20 different endings that you can get. A lot of them are mutually exclusive so keeping regular backups will ensure you get all of them. You also do not have to play the entire 2 and a half years to get the ending achievements. You can use a button combination to skip to the end of the game when going to bed. By holding L + R + Select + Start while the screen fades, when the screen reappears, you will see all of the endings you currently are eligible for as well as all of your stats, such as happiness, cow affection and ranch master score. It's recommended that you do this every so often to check your stats and ensure you're on track even with rich presence. Also bear in mind that if any of these keys are set up as a hotkey, the button combination won't work so be sure to remap your hotkeys to get this to work.

The achievement logic is also set up in a way that each ending achievement will only unlock near the end of the month and the beginning of the next month. I've found that going to bed starting the 28th will unlock the achievements all the way until roughly the 4th or 5th of the next month. If you're struggling to get the achievements to unlock, try using the ending code during this time frame and you should see the achievements pop. The development rate and ranch master achievements don't seem to be affected by this from my experience but the actual ending achievements do.

## Tool Upgrades

In Harvest Moon for the SNES, you find tool upgrades rather than upgrade them at the blacksmith like in later games. Also unlike future games in the series, once you get an upgraded tool, you can only use its upgraded ability rather than choosing which level you want to use. This increases the stamina usage but most of the time, it's a good thing.

Each of the upgraded tools come from a random event on your farm aside from the sprinkler. Once you pass them, the tool will become available the next day. If you fail the event, the upgraded tool becomes purchasable at the tool shop. However, if you want to get them for free and get all the achievements, you'll have to respond accordingly. ## Power Berries & Stamina

When you begin the game, you start with 100 stamina. Each normal tool use uses 2 stamina each time, including refilling the watering can and throwing seeds to plant them. However, you can increase your stamina by finding power berries. Each power berry increases your max stamina by 10 points and there are ten of them in the game. ## Misc Tips

* Take advantage of the infinite nights to do your chores. All of your planting, watering, grass cutting, wood chopping and field clearing should be done exclusively at night even when you're married. The exceptions to this are harvesting grown crops to sell and animals since time freezes in buildings. The -2 affection with your wife can easily be rectified with a gift, whether that's a flower from the mountain or a wild grape or even an egg. Once your animals are taken care of, grown crops become the priority.

* Use the day time to fish in Spring, Summer and Fall as well as harvest forageables and take care of purchases. Fish are worth a whopping 300g each and you can catch 2 of them a day before the shipper comes if you're lucky. Use the day time to also increase your relationships with each of the bachelorettes but save any non harvesting work until after nightfall.

* You can restore your stamina at the hot springs in the mountains. Unlike other games, it's best to jump in and out as each jump restores 20 stamina.  You can also tell how much stamina your character has by his animations after using tools. If he wipes his brow, you have 50% left. If he slumps over and breathes heavy, you're at 25%. If he falls down, you're at 10%. When he closes his eyes and collapses, you're out of stamina. Unlike future games, this means you cannot work until you restore your stamina, either from the food you eat at noon, edible forageables the hot springs.

* Save any grown crops for the next day if you can't harvest them by 5 PM. Anything thrown in the shipping bin after the shipper comes is lost but grown crops will remain grown the next day unless it's Fall, in which case they will wither.

* If you want to get some extra cash going into Summer or work on the spring crops more, you can plant spring crops on Spring 30th and they will continue to grow into Summer until they can be harvested.

* Unless you're going for the Happy Bachelor ending, never buy more than one chicken and never breed cows. When a cow becomes pregnant, it will take either 21 days or a medicine to give birth during which time you cannot milk the cow. If you use the medicine, the baby will effectively cost the same as buying a new cow. When the calf is born, it will take 14 days to become a child and an additional 21 days to grow into an adult. When you buy a cow from the livestock dealer, it will already be a child. The only real benefits are that you get a +70 to your happiness bonus, the main way we will farm happiness as a bachelor, and the pregnant cow gets +10 affection points. However, we will easily farm happiness points when married and the affection boost for the cow can be easily obtained with 2 and a half days of brushing and talking, which isn't worth the 14 additional days we will have to wait to start milking it.

* You can clear tilled tiles by using a small rock or lumber. This is especially useful once you get the golden hoe to arrange your crops in optimal formations.

* You cannot walk over grown crops so the ideal pattern to plant them is in a horseshoe pattern. To maximize your stamina, plant crops in 3x3 squares but remove the middle square from the top row. This will make a U shape with eight squares for crops. From there, you'll want to till the group directly below it and leave a row empty between the two squares. However, for the bottom group of crops, remove the square from the middle of the bottom row instead of the top. This will be the same shape but upside down. This will optimize your watering, especially once you get the sprinkler. While standing in the hole of the top group, we can water the 5 crops surrounding us. We can then move to the row between the two crop groups and water the 6 crops in both bottom rows. We can then finish watering the remaining 5 crops by standing in the gap of the bottom group, watering all 16 squares in just three uses of the sprinkler. Plant additional seed groups using this blueprint of two groups and you can maximize your tool usage and water large numbers of crops in minutes.

* Make regular save backups. Save location varies by your emulator but the game will be named something along the lines of Harvest Moon (USA).srm or whatever the title of your game is. I made a folder tree with years, seasons and days along with notes of anything pertinent that happened. For example, Summer 25 Married Ann, Fall 4th 2nd house upgrade done. This will make it easy to restore specific backups later, especially if you realize you missed something. You will want to back up as frequently as you're comfortable with but it should be daily in Summer to prevent hurricanes.

* When shipping, you do not receive your money until the next morning, even after the shipper picks it up.

* Do not put your animals outside! Unlike future games which track this, there is no benefit to putting your animals outside as it makes your farm lag like crazy, you'll have to gather them back inside at night and they gain no affection points or additional happiness from being outside. In fact, your cows risk getting sick which hurts your happiness and your chickens can die if you have broken fences on the property, which you shouldn't, so leave them inside where they're safe and happy.

* Rain only waters crops in the morning! As random as it sounds, if you plant crops or harvest regrowing crops during the rain, you will have to water them despite the rain.

## Summer 1

Welcome to the new season! The colors have changed to brighter colors, warm clouds hover overhead and we have new crop options! You'll also notice on the mountain that the squirrel no longer appears and that wild grapes have been replaced with a melon further up near the top, making it harder to forage for money. But what we lack in foraging we will make up tenfold in crops sales. Summer crops, unlike spring crops, regrow every three days which means our return on investment skyrockets. There are also no festivals in summer and this is likely due to a new threat that can shut down your entire farm for one day: hurricanes.

Every day, there's a random chance the weather for the next day will be a hurricane. It can rain in summer but a hurricane will show a surprised person in a raincoat with an upturned umbrella talking about the 'hurricane forecast'. If this happens, it will destroy crops, trash your field and force you to go to bed early without allowing you to work on the farm or tend to your animals. If you have cows, this will also mean a hit to your happiness and their affection. They're all around bad so for that purpose, we will be backing up our save files daily and checking the weather every morning. If you see a hurricane, restore the previous days backup as the weather report generates after you go to bed. It sucks losing a day of progress but its better than the alternative.

Our biggest goal for Summer is to upgrade our house at least once by the end of the season if you haven't already. Preferably, we want to start the house upgrade no later than Summer 14th which is a Monday. It will take five days to upgrade. The first day is asking the carpenter to come over and he will mention he'll stop by the house the next day. The second day, he stops by, asks for the resources and once confirmed, he mentions he will start the actual upgrade the next day. From there, it takes three days of working to fully upgrade. This will give us the clock as well as have our upgraded house ready by Summer 19th, which will make the blue feather available for purchase that day. Then we can propose on Summer 25th, or Summer 26th if you're marrying Nina, which is the latest day you can marry and have two anniversaries.

For today, we have the following list of goals.

1. Check the weather for hurricanes.
2. Purchase corn and tomatoes from the flower shop.
3. Fish until sundown.
4. Plant only tomato crops.
5. Water all crops.
6. Start daily backups of our save files to prevent hurricanes.

Immediately check the weather for the next day when you wake up on Summer 1st. If you're unlucky enough to have a hurricane on your first day, reload your Spring 30th backup. If it's good, back up your save file. For the entire month, I had a folder labeled "Summer Year 1" with subfolders for each day that I would backup good save files into.

If the weather appears normal, head into town and purchase corn and tomatoes. Get as much as you want as we will have our first tomato harvests on the 10th and 13th which is plenty of time for the house upgrade if you haven't yet got it. Once you have all the crops you want, head back and fish until sundown to regain some money.

When you get back to the ranch, plant only the tomato seeds. Keep planting them in the optimal pattern outlined in the hints section. We will be unlocking the sprinkler this month on the 20th, which will be an immediate purchase, and having an optimal watering pattern ready will save a ton of time. Since the sprinkler does not run out of water, this will cut your watering time down to a fraction of what is currently is.

You may be wondering why we don't plant the corn seeds tonight. Tomato seeds take 9 days to grow and regrow every 3 days. This will give you crop yields on the 10th, 13th, 16th, 19th, 22st, 25th and 28th. However, if you plant corn as well, that takes 12 days to grow and regrows every 3 days. That would make it ready on the 13th, 16th, 19th, 22nd, 25th and 28th. See the problem? Without the horse, your time is limited and you will struggle to harvest all the crops in time. So by staggering the planting of corn, you can change that to the 14th, 17th, 23rd, 26th and 29th which should give you plenty of time to harvest and give you close to a daily profit starting on the 13th.

Make sure you water your crops, including any spring crops you have planted and cut any grown grass. Then head to bed, save and check the weather. If it's good, back up your current save. If there's a hurricane, reload your Summer 1st backup and redo the day.

Use the miracle potion on your cow and they'll look happy if it successfully took. Switch to the medicine and use it on the cow immediately afterwards. For some reason, pregnancy is coded similarly to a sickness so when you use the medicine, it 'cures it' while still leaving the toggle for pregnancy. This ultimately means when you check the cow the next day, it will be pregnant and move the birthing stable to the right of the door where it needs to be fed. But when you talk to them, the game says that they are past due and ready for birth. This allows you to skip the entirety of the 21 day pregnancy and the cow will give birth just two days after giving it the miracle potion. When the calf is born, you'll be asked to name it, get a whopping +70 happiness points and an achievement we avoided during our ranch master playthrough.

If you've got the house upgrades and were diligently farming the squirrel, you'll likely already be halfway through and be above 400 points. Each baby calf brings in +70 points so use your tomato and corn money to buy additional miracle potions and medicine. Using this method, it's still 5000g per cow but there's no grass check and you can safely use the glitch to breed new calves every two days which is the crux of our strategy for getting this ending.

Also don't forget that around the middle of summer, a random event will break the tree in your farm again. Enter it, talk to the harvest sprite and tell him "Yes" when he asks if you use your sickle for +20 happiness and the golden sickle upgrade.

If you want an additional boost in happiness, feel free to purchase chickens to go with your cows. They will grant a flat +10 happiness each, only cost 1000g and don't need to be fed to maintain happiness. Don't breed them this time as breeding them only gives +5. However, the livestock dealer will refuse to sell them to you if you don't have enough grass planted and cows are much more important at this point in time.

Once you're ready, head into Fall, back up your save and do the ending trick to check your happiness. You should hopefully be over 500 at this point between upgrades, cow breeding and chickens. If you've been diligent with house upgrades, breeding cows and checking for the squirrel, it's very possible you're close or at 800 happiness for the ending.

## Fall Goals

1. Breed cows.
2. Continue fishing for happiness and money.
3. Don't forget to feed the Harvest Sprite a mushroom for +20 happiness.
4. Bring a fish to the harvest festival for a power berry.
5. Win the egg festival for either a +30 or +80 boost.
6. Wrap up and get the achievement before the end of the season.

Fall goals remain simple. Fish for money, purchase additional miracle potions and medicine for baby calves and check for the Harvest Sprite to get another +20 happiness and tool upgrade. If you manage to breed at least 5 cows, you'll gain a whopping +350 happiness points which is essentially half the score you need on it's own when combined with the original 50 we had when we started. Be sure to check for the fallen harvest sprite in the beginning of the season and give him a mushroom for +20 happiness and the golden hoe. Don't forget about the Fullmoon Berry growing on the summit on Fall 10th through the 12th that sells for 600g a piece! That will go nicely towards additional miracle potions.

On the 12th, either catch a fish or take the Fullmoon Berry and bring it to the Harvest Festival for the power berry. This is another +10 to your happiness. Keep buying miracle potions and medicine to breed cows when you can afford it and head to the Egg Festival on the 20th, backing up your save beforehand. When you win, you'll get either the Turtle Shell for +80 happiness or the power berry for +30 happiness. You'll gain another +10 happiness from eating the power berry the next day.

You can also contemplate selling a chicken for the power berry. But bear in mind that you will ultimately lose 10 happiness doing this. You can repurchase the chicken which will cancel out the happiness loss but unless you need the additional stamina, it's not worth it.

As you near the end of the month on the 28th, save your game, back it up and check your happiness. Chances are, you've likely hit the goal of 800 and you'll see the exclusive ending, which features our character chopping wood and eating a power berry, unlocking the following achievement.

## Winter Goals - Optional

1. Continue breeding cows.
2. Get Eve, Nina and Ann's affection to over 200 points.
3. Attend the Thanksgiving festival for +50 points each.
4. Get the golden axe and power berry for +20 and +10 happiness.
5. Get the golden hammer for +20 happiness.
6. Keep an eye out for the fortune teller's daughter. Give her an egg for +30 happiness.

If you still haven't gotten the achievement, Winter will have plenty more boosts for you to get and push you over the 800 requirement. For our daily activities, continue to breed cows with the miracle potion and medicine so that we get +70 happiness each time. You'll also have to start feeding most of them as a lot of them will be fully grown at this point. You can buy the milker for 1800g if you want but very likely the cows will only be producing small milk for 150g each. You'll want to get the remaining three girls to over 200 points of affection by answering their questions or drinking with Eve before the 10th as well. Then, when Thanksgiving rolls around on the 10th, talk to all five girls in town. Each girl will give you a cake and you'll get +10 happiness from each one for a total of +50 happiness.

Once the earthquake happens, you can also take your axe to the small pond on the mountain behind the boulder and trade it for the golden axe for +20 more happiness. Be sure to use it on the stump and grab the power berry for +10 more happiness. The carpenter will also ask you to borrow your hammer in this time frame. Let him for +20 happiness and the golden hammer.

Also, be on the lookout for the fortune teller's daughter when you wake up. She comes by the farm when your chickens are laying eggs and asks for one. Give it to her and you'll get +30 happiness. These boosts, combined with the +70 happiness per calf, will be more than enough to push you over the 800 happiness threshold. Be sure to continue feeding your cows until the 28th and use the ending trick. You'll see the exclusive new ending and pop the achievement.

# 5) Relaxing for the Final Achievement

Now that you've completed the game, it's time to get the final achievement which is essentially for doing nothing. To get it, you have to have no animals, happiness under 100, be unmarried and be under a threshold for the bachelorettes so you don't accidentally trigger a different ending. To that end, we'll start a new game in the second file slot.

Go through the tutorial, talking to each of the villagers and return home. Then, you'll want to immediately go to bed. Sleep until the 28th and use the ending code to check the ending. You'll see your character run off the farm while your dog barks. You'll pop the final achievement too. Congratulations on your mastery or completion!
