# Episode 32 - Pedal to the Metal Gear

In a recent episode we covered Capcom's beloved Resident Evil, a series spanning 30 years of horror and thrills - and a genre that many other developers have taken a liking to in the "survival horror" world. There is however another series from an entirely different genre approaching their own 30 year mark which modern developers have shied away from - A game where stealth and outside the box thinking provides the player with several approaches to tackle the goal, and a series that frequently goes beyond what we traditionally expect to see in a video game. 

I speak of course of the Metal Gear Solid series - Launched in 1998 (Not to be confused with regular Metal Gear) to critical acclaim, Kojima would dazzle us. From the classic that is Psycho Mantis reading your memory card, to alternative ways to defeat the bosses of Snake Eater; all the way into the unfortunately unreleased PT and more  - Kojima carved himself a space on the mount everest of gaming that would appear to remain eternal. 

For exactly that reason I have my guest here tonight, {% rauserpic WilHiteWarrior %}, developer for nearly every RA Metal Gear game including a handful of spinoffs or prototypes, to dig in on what makes this series and creator so special for those of us here at RA, and beyond.
