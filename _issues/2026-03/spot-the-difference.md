Hajime Hinata is a new student of Hope's Peak Academy, an elite school which opens every path in life after graduation. When arriving at the school he meets his new classmates in their classroom, but shortly after they all realize that none of them can remember how they even entered the room nor is anyone able to leave it. Just a moment later a magical rabbit creature appears and in a blink of an eye they find themselves on a tropical island. Shortly after a so called "school trip" becomes a fight for life and death and Hajime needs to solve murder cases to escape this nightmare. Can you help him keeping a clear mind and find all 10 differences?
