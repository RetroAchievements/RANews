### 🧠 General Advice (Before Starting a New Game)

When starting a Championship, you cannot quit and resume later despite saving, it will make you restart and do it in a single session.

> Start the first championship as Jimbo to unlock some other achievements for completing certain races as a specific character [such as this one](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/557689).
