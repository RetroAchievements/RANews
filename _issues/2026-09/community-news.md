## SpringRO Open Beta

The open beta for SpringRO is almost here!

The beta for SpringRO will run from September 18 to Friday, October 9.

Following the end of the beta, the servers will come back online for the full release on: Friday, October 9, 2026 9:00 PM
When the servers launch for full release, all beta progress will be wiped out.

## Site Updates

### RAWeb 2026.09.17

**New Features**
- We've started rolling out our ticket system rewrite, beginning with a new tickets list page and a "tickets inbox" page. More upgrades to the tickets system are on the way - this is the first batch of updates.
- Speaking of tickets, if you're opening a new ticket, you'll be warned if the achievement logic has changed between the time you played and the time you opened the create ticket form.
- The forum will now completely hide posts from users you've blocked.
- The player-submitted screenshots feature is now out of beta. Submit screenshots for our game pages, get a badge. You'll find a new "Contribute" section on the game page's sidebar.

**Highlights for Developers**
- You may notice your developer feed loads a bit more quickly.
- The code notes page now uses pagination to avoid blowing up the server's memory.

[Full release notes](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/RAWeb/releases/tag/2026.09.17)
