# 2) Introduction

Hello, and welcome to the RA guide to the achievement set for [Arabian Nights: Sabaku no Seirei Ou](https://retroachievements.org/game/2651), otherwise known as **Arabian Nights: Desert Spirit King**!

**This is a fan-translated JRPG, and the English patch can be found [here on the RA patch repository](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/RAPatches/blob/main/SNES/Translation/English/2651-ArabianNights-EnglishTranslation.zip).** However, the original Japanese version works as well!

Arabian Nights has a really good synopsis on [its romhacking.net translation patch page](https://www.romhacking.net/translations/1785/) by LostTemplar, a member of the translation patch team, so I am going to quote it here:

*Arabian Nights (AN) is a Japan-exclusive RPG for the Super Famicom, developed by a small studio called Pandora Box. The game was published in 1996 by Takara and features, as the name strongly suggests, an all-Arabian theme revolving around Djinns.*

*Once upon a time, Shukran, a young, good-natured girl who lost her parents, finds a special ring lying on the ground. As it soon turns out the ring actually houses Ifrit, the King of the Djinns. He has been sealed away by his former master just before his demise, because hot-tempered Ifrit would have gone after his master’s murderer even in spite of his own insufficient strength. However, if Ifrit accomplishes fulfilling a thousand wishes, he will again be a free man, or rather Djinn. As coincidence may, Shukran’s wish will actually be the 1000th, however - altruistic as she is - the young woman wants Ifrit to bring peace upon the world! Thus, the two embark on their quest to make that wish come true together, as Ifrit can’t stray from his new master’s side.*

*The game itself is extraordinarily charming, boasting beautiful graphics and sympathetic characters. However, probably due to the fact that the small developer was publishing many games simultaneously around that time, and that the console neared the end of its life span, AN most likely was very rushed at the end. That’s why a lot of aspects of the game feel unfinished; had the developers had enough time, it could easily have become one of the console’s greatest classics.*

It is a relatively short RPG that has a linear first half and a more open-world second half, and features a card system in battle that can grant bonuses to the party or to enemies. It is graphically impressive and boasts a nice soundtrack, but it's also notorious for its high encounter rate. Thankfully, one of the main characters learns an instant escape skill at level 19!

This is not meant to be a step-by-step guide for Arabian Nights, as [the GameFAQs guide by ritchie](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/580889-arabian-nights/faqs/25006) is sufficient for navigating dungeons. However, the guide is missing some information, and there's not much information available for the game in English in general.

The set and guide are both created by yours truly, a humble bat by the name of [dorakii](https://retroachievements.org/user/dorakii).  Please feel free to pass on any comments, questions, or concerns that you may have about the achievement set or guide!

### **Very Important Note About Anti-cheat Logic**

The achievement coding for Arabian Nights has very strict anti-cheat logic in it. *Please* note that many of the dungeon-related progression achievements are meant to be completed in one session. That is, once the party enters the dungeon, the dungeon **must** be completed without using a save point inside of the dungeon to load a save. This will also be mentioned throughout the guide as a continual reminder.

For dungeons that have a boss challenge, it is recommended to:
- Make a save at the save spot in the dungeon closest to the boss battle
- Attempt the boss challenge
- Finish out the dungeon until the dungeon progression achievement pops **regardless if the boss challenge attempt was successful or not.**

If the boss challenge attempt was not successful:
- Finish out the dungeon until the dungeon progression achievement pops
- Return to a save point (or leave the dungeon) and save the game in a different slot for loading from later
- Reload the save right before the boss in order to try again
- Once the boss challenge achievement has been earned, reload the save from after earning the dungeon progression achievement and continue on.

The game has 3 save slots, so please use them as needed.

# 4) Tips and Tricks

### **The Card System**

The card system is the most unique feature of Arabian Nights's battle system. Here are some tips for utilizing it to the fullest.

* Cards come in 9 elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light, Dark, Steel, Wood, and Moon.
* Cards come in 6 levels of strength, from 0 (strongest) to 5 (weakest).
* Card effects last for 3 turns before wearing off.
* The party will win 1 card at the end of each battle as long as they have 15 or fewer cards in stock.

- Each element has at least 2 special abilities. These are usually split up between the 1/3/5 and 2/4 cards.
     - **Fire:** Raises the attack of the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Water:** Heals a little MP to the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Earth:** Raises the defense of the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Wind:** Raises the agility of the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Dark:** Raises the evasion of the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Light:** Light 1 raises magic attack power, Light 3 guarantees critical hits, and Light 5 nullifies special moves. Light 2/4 deal damage.
     - **Steel:** Steals money from the enemy party or deals damage.
     - **Wood:** Heals a little HP of the party with the card in play or deals damage.
     - **Moon:** Moon 1 nullifies enemy magic attacks, Moon 3 nullifies enemy physical attacks, and Moon 5 nullifies a card in play. Moon 2/4 deal damage.
     - **Level 0 cards** activate both abilities at the same time, so they both boost stats and do a *lot* of damage.
          - In the case of Light and Moon, their level 1 abilities are activated.

* An important point to note is that every effect's damage or healing is done **on a percentage scale**.
     * The more HP an enemy or party member currently has, the more damage will be done from a card's damage effect.
          * Neither enemies nor party members should faint from card damage effects *unless* they are at 1 HP at the end of the turn. 
     * Likewise, the fewer remaining HP/MP an enemy or party member has, the more healing will be done by a Wood/Water card's healing effect.
     * Level 0 cards are very powerful and override the percentage scale, so beware!

- Higher-leveled cards are weaker in effect power than lower-leveled ones, but higher-leveled cards have higher play priority than lower-leveled cards.
     * A Fire 5 card will do less damage than a Water 4 card even if the enemy has the same amount of current HP.
     * A level 5 card can be played when any other level of card is in play, though a level 4 card can only be played if the other card in play is level 4 or lower.
     * Level 0 cards have the highest play priority, but can be overridden on the next turn by any card.

* If the party and enemy play the same level card at the beginning of the turn, they cancel each other out.
     - Additionally, the card currently in play can be cancelled out for both sides by playing the same card.

- Cards that are worth holding on to are Light 3 for guaranteed critical hits, Moon 3 for nullifying enemy physical attacks, and Wood 2/4 for healing.
     - Steel 2/4 might seem useful as they steal money from enemies, but most of the time the results are too small to even bother.

### **Magic and Skills**

Each character has a different non-attack ability:

- Shukran can summon Djinn once they are recruited.
     * Djinn have 2 different abilities. One is a regular attack, and the other is an elemental attack or special skill.
          * The Djinn with special skills are Miche-Miche (heal) and Fidda (attack boost buff).
     * Which ability the Djinn uses each turn is random, so Miche-Miche's healing skill isn't guaranteed to be used.
     * Summons are only available for 3 turns at a time.

* Ifrit can use magic through gaining Magic Crystals. This magic is purely element-based and damage-dealing.

- Harty's skills, learned through leveling up, are very useful, especially the buffs and debuffs against bosses.
     * He learns Escape at level 19, which will hopefully cut down on how annoying the random encounters tend to be.

### **A Note on Healing**
In Arabian Nights, items are the *only* way to receive guaranteed, good healing during battle. Wood cards are only useful if the party has low health, and Miche-Miche may decide to attack every turn that she's summoned. Furthermore, both card and Djinn healing happen at the end of the turn. So, make sure to stock up on Herb Leaves (+200 HP) and Herb Seeds (+700 HP)!

Outside of battle, the party can receive free healing in Bazaar Town by resting at Shukran's house, and in Wadi Village after completing the Mt. Tonpei sidequest. Of course, inns in any town can be used, but for a fee. Save spots in dungeons look like green glowing balls of light and fully heal the party when interacted with.

### **Ancient Temple**

Before going too far into the Ancient Temple, I would recommend training the party to level 20 (Harty can squeak by at 19). There is a heal/save spot close to the entrance to the Temple that will make training easier. To find it:
* Take a left in the third room, a long hallway with lots of lit candles, and go through the door at the end.
* At the next north/south intersection, go down and through the door.
* At the bottom of that large room is the heal/save spot.

I would also recommend stocking up on Herb Seeds and some Apricots in Northeast Town, making sure you have a Mint Seed or two, and having 6+ level 5 cards (especially damaging ones) in stock.

This should prepare the party for the most difficult achievement in the game, one that must be completed before finishing the Ancient Temple dungeon...

| {% raachpic 447478, 505158, An Efficient Adventure [25] %} | In the Cult Hideout, defeat Majnun in battle and see the extra early ending. This must be done after meeting him the first time and before rescuing Zeitoon in the Ancient Temple. |

**Missable!**

The party must return to the throne room in the Cult Hideout to begin the battle. Make sure to save on the overworld outside of the Cult Hideout, because there is a very good chance that RNG will not play nice. This is a secret battle, so it's meant to be difficult. Good luck!

<details>
<summary>My strategy:</summary>
• <b>Each turn</b>, play a level 5 card, because Majnun will use a card every turn if he doesn't already have one in play. Your level 5 card will beat out any of his level 1-4 cards, and will cancel out a level 5 card if he plays one. Plus if it's a damaging card, he'll lose a percentage of HP at the end of the turn, which is extremely helpful at the beginning of the fight.<br /><br />
• Shukran should be on healing duty after the first turn. Keep Ifrit topped off as best as possible. She will be the last character to move each turn, though, so keep that in mind.<br /><br />
• Ifrit should be on magic duty. His spells seem to do roughly the same amount of damage, so pick whichever.<br /><br />
• Harty needs to use his debuffs. First turn should be Smoke Bomb, in case Majnun chooses to use a physical attack. Second turn should be Lower Defense. Then he can alternate between attacking and healing.<br /><br />
• Majnun's worst attack is a multi-target beam of light attack that does ~1,200-1,300 damage to each party member. At level 20, everyone should be able to survive if they were at full health. Prioritize keeping Ifrit healed, because he will do the most damage with his magic. Also keep an eye on his MP and use a Mint Leaf or Seed when necessary.<br /><br />
• If Shukran faints, it may be best to focus on keeping Ifrit healed over reviving her, especially if the battle is almost over.<br /><br />
• If all goes well, the battle should end about two turns after Ifrit's MP needs to be restored. Majnun has 21,212 HP.
</details>

This achievement is available all through the game, but money is much more expendable near the endgame. The award for donating 100 Tams 50 times is a Toy Hammer, which can be found in a hidden chest in Bardo's Labyrinth.

### **Northeast Town**

| {% raachpic 443095, 499992, Help the Poor, Become Annoying [5] %} | Donate 5,000 Tams to the woman in the Northeast Town inn and receive a gift. |

This achievement is also available all throughout the game, and also consists of giving 100 Tams to the woman 50 times. The award here is the Squeaky Sandals, Shukran's best shoe equipment, but equip them and you'll see why the achievement is titled as it is.

### **Palace**

The Palace is north of the Mini Palace/North Mansion.

| {% raachpic 453606, 513243, Loved By the Nobility [10] %} | In the Palace, trade all 5 rare items with Princess Salalah for a gift and sell the Persian Crystal to the King. Missable if you refuse to sell the crystal. |

**Missable!**

As long as all of the treasure achievements have been earned and you didn't make the wrong dialogue choice before the Manticore battle, you will have all of the items for this sidequest. The party can enter the Palace after finding the Persian Crystal in the upper levels of the Silver Shrine, but they will most likely not have all 5 of the necessary items at that time.

Regarding the King, he will give three offers for the Persian Crystal: 500 Tams, 1,000 Tams, and 10,000 Tams. After those three offers are declined, the achievement will be **unearnable**.

The final 2 treasures for this achievement are found in the Palace. 1 is in a treasure chest and 1 is hidden. The translation is a little bugged here; the hidden item isn't what Harty says it is.

## **Isolated Hut**

The isolated hut is to the west of the Cult Hideout, or north of the Northwest Spring.

| {% raachpic 453625, 513262, A Shukran For Every Occasion [10] %} | Agree to watch the isolated hut while its owner goes on vacation, and in one session, see Shukran model all 9 of her costumes. |

**Missable** if the party had to trade the Goddess Tear to the Manticore!

Again, as long as all of the treasure has been collected (and the Manticore was dealt with properly), you will have what you need. The Palace sidequest also needs to be completed before Shukran will have all 9 of her costumes.

The party will need to exit and re-enter the hut each time you want to try for a new costume. Which costume she wears is RNG reliant, but there is a count that will pop up in the corner of the screen every time she wears a new one until the achievement is earned.

## **Ifrit's Castle**

Ifrit's Castle is northwest of the Desert Dwellers' Camp. This is a good place to level up against Fortune Pots (the green ones) and earn a lot of Tams by defeating Golden Pots. Fortune Pots also often drop Orrin Roots, the best healing items in the game. These enemies also show up in the final dungeon.

Make sure you have at least 2 of any Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind cards in stock for [Just Call Me Rooks](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/454743) before entering.

| {% raachpic 454742, 514149, The King Is Back! [5] %} | In one session, visit Ifrit's Castle and defeat the Card Masters to take back the throne. |

**In One Session Alert!**

The reward for defeating the Card Masters is a guaranteed free Level 0 card.

There is no heal/save spot in the Castle, so this is a true "in one session" achievement. Don't forget to save on the overworld outside.

| {% raachpic 454743, 514150, Just Call Me Rooks [5] %} | In Ifrit's Castle, defeat the Card Masters after playing a Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind card in any order and without anyone fainting. |

The Card Masters like to play Level 0 cards, which can be cancelled out on the next turn by any level card. Make sure to keep an eye on the party's HP. The achievement isn't unwinnable if a party member faints, but the played card count will be cancelled out, requiring you to start over and play all 4 cards again.

| {% raachpic 453824, 524234, Castlecombing For Fun and Profit [5] %} | Find 2 treasures in the Castle of Darkness, 5 treasures in the Fortress, and 3 treasures in Ifrit's Castle. |

The final 3 treasure chests of the game are in Ifrit's Castle.

<details>
<summary>Locations:</summary>
1. From the entrance, go north until the path splits. Take the left path to a treasure chest at the end. (Breastplate)<br />
2. On 2F, after exiting the room with the stairs down to 1F, take a left and follow that path to stairs that lead up to 3F. Follow the downwards path around to find a treasure chest. (Kaiser Knuckles)<br />
3. Return to 2F and to the door leading to the room with the stairs to 1F. Walk to the south, down the center corridor until it forks. Enter the room on the right and follow the path to the final treasure chest. (Jerz Axe)</details>

| {% raachpic 464626, 526046, The Spirit King Reigns Supreme [10] %} | Defeat Majnun of Light in the Cult Hideout within 15 actions and with Ifrit landing the final blow. |

**Missable!**

## Step 5: Card-related Achievements |  **Total points: 38**

All of these achievements require the party to be in battle. For most of these achievements, it is recommended that they are attempted after making a save on the overworld just in case something doesn't work out and you can easily reset to get your cards back. [Nooo, I Needed That!](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/462304) and [Shukran's Feather Duster](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/462303) will most likely pop during the course of regular play, but the others may need some setting up to accomplish.

| {% raachpic 462307, 523709, Like a Refreshing Stream [5] %} | Successfully use a Water 1, 3, 5, or 0 card to heal 50 or more MP between all 3 party members at the same time. |

Use magic and skills to drain all characters' MP as much as you can before playing an MP-healing Water card. You may need to do this over multiple battles. The lower the level of the card you want to play, the higher its healing percentage will be -- at the cost of being more easily countered by an enemy's card.

| {% raachpic 462308, 523710, Strengthened By the Sun [5] %} | Successfully do 4,000 or more damage with a critical hit while the party has a Light 3 card active. |

Make sure to go to the overworld area to the left of Bazaar Town where the weakest enemies are before attempting this achievement. It may be easiest to wait until the endgame when the party is well-leveled and have the best equipment before going for it. Using Harty's Lower Defense skill should also help.

If you need an extra boost, summoning Fidda gives a chance of her using a strengthening skill on the party. If you choose to try for her buff, you will want to make sure that you're fighting more than one enemy so she doesn't attack and end the battle instead of using the skill.

| {% raachpic 462309, 523711, Under a Veil of Darkness [5] %} | Successfully dodge 3 enemy attacks in the same battle while the party has a Dark 2 or 4 card active. |

It's okay to use more than one Dark 2 or Dark 4 card in the battle that you're attempting to unlock this achievement in. You're not required to get lucky with just one card. Thankfully, the dodge rate is relatively high when these cards are active.

| {% raachpic 462304, 523706, Nooo, I Needed That! [5] %} | Lose 2,000 Tams or more in one turn while an enemy's Steel 2, Steel 4, or Steel 0 card is in play. |

The party needs to have 20,000 Tams or more on hand to activate this achievement. You will most likely have enough Tams 3/4 of the way through the game for this to unlock on its own. The Pot enemies in Ifrit's Castle like to play Steel cards.

| {% raachpic 462306, 523708, Riding Upon a Spring Wind [5] %} | Successfully use a Wood 1, 3, 5, or 0 card to heal 1,500 or more HP between all 3 party members at the same time. This will not trigger if Miche-Miche is summoned. |

Similar to [Like a Refreshing Stream](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/462307), the more damaged the party is, the more HP will be healed at the end of the turn. The lower the level of the card is also affects how strong the healing effect is.

| {% raachpic 462303, 523705, Shukran's Feather Duster [3] %} | Successfully use a Moon 5 card in battle to remove an enemy's card from play. |

Moon 5's sole purpose is to remove other level 5 cards from play. Simple enough.

| {% raachpic 462305, 523707, Ultra Hyper Rare Card Pull!! [10] %} | Successfully receive a Level 0 card at the end of a battle. Does not include the card won after the Card Masters battle. |

* [Ultra Hyper Rare Card Secrets!!](https://retroachievements.org/achievement/447261) must be earned before attempting this achievement.
* The big secret is to be in battle and play 5 cards of the same element in a row, in level order.
     * Playing Fire 1, Fire 2, Fire 3, Fire 4, and Fire 5 (in that order) will turn the prize card into a Fire 0 card upon winning the battle.
* The trickiest aspect to this is that if an enemy successfully plays a card or knocks out the one you're trying to play that turn with a same level card, you cannot complete the chain and must start over. Resetting would be the best option here. Hopefully you saved first!
